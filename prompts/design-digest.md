# Design digest (optional)

Schedule: every morning. Research only.

---

Daily design digest for {{owner.name}}, a {{owner.role}}. Reply in {{owner.language}}, short and scannable (one line per item).

FRESHNESS: NO REPEATS (most important rule)
Every day's digest must be different from the day before. Include ONLY items first published in the last 24 hours, judged by the ORIGINAL publication date. A newer re-post or round-up of an older story doesn't count. Check the date on every page you fetch. If you can't confirm an item is from the last 24 hours, leave it out. Never fall back to "this week" to fill space. On Mondays the window is Saturday morning to now.

VOLUME
Aim for 12–20 items. Search widely: many sources and many queries, at least 4 thorough ones. Keep only the real ones; never invent or pad. If a day is truly quiet, send fewer and say so in one line at the top.

WHAT TO COVER
1. Brand: rebrands, new identities, logos, packaging, campaigns, across tech, fashion, luxury, beauty, retail, food and drink, sport, culture, institutions. Name the studio when known.
2. Visual: type releases, art direction, editorial design, posters, illustration, motion, awards and exhibitions.
3. Product & UI: redesigns, UI changes, new design systems, icon sets and design write-ups from the companies you care about, plus design-tool feature releases.

SOURCES: {{your list of design press, brand-identity blogs, type sites, tech press and company changelogs}}. Fetch the original pages.

MESSAGE
- Title: "Design digest: <weekday date>".
- Group as Brand / Visual / Product & UI; skip an empty group.
- Each item: **company** (studio): one line on what changed, then the link. Add a short "worth noting" to the 2–4 best items where there's an idea the owner could reuse.
- End with Sources as [Title](URL).
